### 1. [Semifactual Credit-Augmented Policy Optimization](<http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40360v1>)

**arXiv ID：** 2609.40360

**作者：** Junshu Pan、Zhizhang Fu、Shulin Huang、Yiran Ding、Zifan Cheng、Wenqi Shao、Qiaosheng Zhang、Yue Zhang

**arXiv 学科：** cs.LG、cs.AI、cs.CL

[论文页面](<http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40360v1>) · [PDF](<https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.40360v1>)

**研究分类：** METHOD · 方法迁移

**优先级：** P1

**分类把握：** 中

**主题标签：** RLVR、credit assignment、token-level advantage、semifactual intervention、GRPO、spurious dependence、training signal design

**核心机制：** 用「semifactual prompt 干预」——保持问题与答案不变、只改动任务无关的提示特征——测量固定回复下 token 概率漂移，得到 token 级稳定性分数；再把归一化稳定性注入 GRPO 的 token 级优势分配，在训练早期压低高漂移 token 的优势，且稳定性本身不额外加分（纯文本 RLVR 的信用分配改造，不涉及视觉状态生成）。

**追踪理由：** LVR 主线同样面临「轨迹训练中的贡献分配」瓶颈：latent 推理轨迹中哪些步骤是真正必要的、哪些只是与答案共现的虚假依赖，目前缺少可操作的诊断与训练信号。该论文提供了「保持答案不变的干预 → 度量表示漂移 → 转化为优势权重」的可复用范式模板，且明确指出「注意力/概率稳定不等于因果贡献」，与 LVR 中「probe 可读不等于答案使用」的边界判断一致。摘要级材料足以判断机制，但未给出视觉或多模态证据。

**与 LVR 的联系：** 可迁移到 latent 轨迹的 credit assignment：把 token 干预换成对 latent 状态的任务无关扰动（如视觉输入中与答案无关的裁剪/背景替换、latent 位置或缓存的非语义替换），度量固定答案下 latent 状态漂移，用其压低「不稳定但被高优势加权」的 latent 步骤。这也贴近「区分内容变化与计算深度/位置/缓存」的诊断需求。

**主要假设与风险：** 核心假设是「干预下的表示稳定性 ≈ 因果有效性」，但在 LVR 中高相似 latent 未必更正确（可能只是坍缩或计算加深）；且论文是纯文本、单模态结论，不能直接外推到 VLM 推理。

**下一步核查：** 1) 读全文确认 semifactual 干预的具体构造与是否真的保持答案不变（尤其是否含人工筛选、是否覆盖视觉模态）；2) 查看更细的消融：仅惩罚高漂移 token 的效果来源、归一化方式、在训练早期之后的调度；3) 验证能否在 VLM/latent 轨迹设定下定义可度量的漂移量，并设计「答案不变」的多模态干预；4) 核对代码仓库是否包含可直接复用的 token 级信用分配实现。

#### 方法迁移分析

**原问题：** RLVR（GRPO）在 LLM 推理中把同一结果优势广播给每个 token，可能同时强化与答案无关的虚假依赖，导致模型对任务无关提示特征敏感，且该敏感性存在显著的 token 级差异。

**可迁移机制：** semifactual 干预（保持问题与答案不变的提示改写）+ 固定回复下的 token 概率漂移度量 + 归一化稳定性分数注入 token 级优势（仅惩罚不稳定、不为稳定加分）。

**LVR 瓶颈：** 推理轨迹训练中的贡献分配：当 LVR 用 latent 状态/轨迹替代显式 token 链时，如何判断哪些 latent 步骤承担真实证据，哪些只是虚假相关或计算冗余；以及信息坍缩下缺少细粒度训练信号。

**拟议迁移：** 假设性迁移：在 latent 推理训练中构造「答案不变」的多模态 semifactual 干预（替换与答案无关的视觉区域、打乱无关实体顺序、对 latent 状态施加保持语义的扰动），用冻结答案下 latent 轨迹的距离/概率漂移作为不稳定度，按与 SCAPO 相同的方式对 latent 级优势做抑制性重加权；可先用于诊断，再用于训练。

**失败条件：** 若 latent 状态没有可定义的输出概率、或漂移主要由计算深度/位置/缓存而非内容决定，则该稳定性信号会退化为噪声；此外多模态干预很难保证「答案确实不变」，一旦干预改变了必要视觉证据，惩罚会错误地压制有效推理步骤。

**title/T1：** Semifactual Credit-Augmented Policy Optimization

**abstract/A1：** Reinforcement learning with verifiable rewards (RLVR) has improved the reasoning capabilities of large language models (LLMs), yet their predictions remain sensitive to task-irrelevant prompt features. We investigate this sensitivity through semifactual prompt interventions that preserve the underlying problem and its answer. Our analysis reveals substantial variation in token-level sensitivity and shows that suppressing high-drift token candidates during decoding improves reasoning accuracy without updating model weights. These findings highlight a limitation of Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), which assigns the same outcome-derived advantage to every response token and may reinforce potential spurious dependence alongside useful reasoning. Motivated by this observation, we introduce Semifactual Credit-Augmented Policy Optimization (SCAPO), a causally inspired variant of GRPO that incorporates semifactual stability into token-level credit assignment. SCAPO measures token probability drift for fixed responses under semifactual interventions and uses normalized stability scores to reduce advantages for relatively unstable tokens during early training, while granting no additional credit for stability alone. On Qwen3-4B-Base and Qwen3-1.7B-Base, SCAPO improves AIME 2024-2026 accuracy over GRPO by 5.63 and 4.17 percentage points, respectively. At both model scales, SCAPO achieves the best results on most evaluated mathematics benchmarks and all evaluated out-of-distribution benchmarks among the compared methods. These results suggest that semifactual stability provides an effective training signal for improving reasoning and generalization through finer-grained credit assignment in RLVR. The code is available at https://github.com/DtYXs/SCAPO.

### 2. [Multimodal Flow: Unified Flow Modeling of Language and Vision in Embedding Spaces](<http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40362v1>)

**arXiv ID：** 2609.40362

**作者：** Hongyuan Tao、Xinggang Wang、Lianghui Zhu、Yongkang Li、Yunchao Wei、Bin Feng、Shaoyu Chen、Qian Zhang、Chang Huang、Kai Yu

**arXiv 学科：** cs.CV

[论文页面](<http://arxiv.org/abs/2609.40362v1>) · [PDF](<https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.40362v1>)

**研究分类：** METHOD · 方法迁移

**优先级：** P2

**分类把握：** 中

**主题标签：** continuous embedding flow、flow matching、chunk-causal backbone、multimodal generative pretraining、parallel chunk prediction、continuous latent representation

**核心机制：** 将文本块与图像视为有序的连续「hyperchunks」，用共享的 chunk-causal flow backbone 通过 Flow Matching 学习单一向量场；训练时并行预测多个目标 chunk，推理时顺序生成 hyperchunk；联合注意力做跨模态交互，模态专属 FFN 分别处理语言与视觉。

**追踪理由：** 摘要给出了完整迁移链所需的关键部件：连续（非量化）多模态表示、若干步向量场采样、以及训练并行／推理顺序的不一致生成设置。这三点恰好对应 LVR 中 latent 状态生成、低步数修正与「生成 latent 假设后再筛选」的机制需求，值得核验其 chunk 并行预测是否可当作多假设生成与修正的骨架。

**与 LVR 的联系：** LVR 主线的一环是「如何生成并修正 latent 推理状态」（Generation），以及保持必要视觉细节的连续预测工作区（Representation）。该工作不研究推理，但在连续空间中同时承载语言顺序结构与视觉空间结构，并具备并行多目标预测与顺序生成两种模式，可作为 latent 工作区表示的候选骨架去检验：连续 chunk 是否比量化 token 保留更多可被答案使用的视觉证据。

**主要假设与风险：** 主要风险是该论文的目标是生成式统一建模（图像生成与多模态打分），而非推理；其 chunk 间的连续流可能只是生成质量的手段，未必形成可被下游答案读取的「推理状态」。若没有显式推理链、无停止/预算控制、且训练目标不涉及答案使用 latent，则迁移仅为结构性假设，证据薄弱。

**下一步核查：** 1）核对全文中的 chunk 因果掩码与「并行预测多目标 chunk」的具体实现（是否共享同一向量场、是否引入 chunk 间依赖）；2）查看是否有中间 chunk 表示被下游任务读取的实验；3）确认是否报告推理期采样步数与顺序生成的开销；4）核验代码与权重是否与摘要声称一致。

#### 方法迁移分析

**原问题：** 统一多模态预训练中，离散图像量化带来视觉量化瓶颈，而「离散语言 + 连续图像」的混合方案需要模态相关的目标与采样流程；论文希望用完全连续的共享生成过程替代两者。

**可迁移机制：** 把文本块与图像组织为有序连续 hyperchunks，用共享 chunk-causal flow backbone 通过 Flow Matching 学习单一向量场；训练时并行预测多个目标 chunk，推理时顺序生成 hyperchunk，并以联合注意力做跨模态交互。

**LVR 瓶颈：** LVR 的 Generation 与 Representation 瓶颈：latent 推理状态应以何种连续表示承载必要视觉细节，以及如何用少量步骤生成并修正该状态，而不是把视觉信息压成量化 token。

**拟议迁移：** 假设：把 hyperchunk 视为可生成的 latent 工作区单元，用 flow 向量场在连续空间生成候选推理状态（并行多目标预测天然对应多假设生成），再以真实视觉证据对候选做筛选与更新；顺序生成模式可类比「先铺开多个候选，再逐步精化」的推理轨迹。该迁移属本人假设，原论文未验证推理用途。

**失败条件：** 若 flow 目标只优化重建／生成似然，训练信号不要求答案读取 latent 内容，则候选状态可能与任务答案脱耦；此外若并行预测缺少 chunk 间条件依赖，多假设之间难以相互校验，且顺序生成步数带来的成本可能抵消精度收益。

**title/T1：** Multimodal Flow: Unified Flow Modeling of Language and Vision in Embedding Spaces

**abstract/A1：** We present Multimodal Flow, a fully continuous generative model of language and vision. Most unified multimodal models either model both language and quantized images as discrete tokens or combine discrete language prediction with continuous image generation. The former introduces a visual quantization bottleneck. The latter requires modality-dependent objectives and sampling procedures. Fully continuous modeling avoids these trade-offs and enables a shared generative process, but remains underexplored for multimodal pretraining. Multimodal Flow introduces a unified continuous architecture that integrates multimodal continuous representations with a shared chunk-causal flow backbone. It organizes text blocks and images as ordered continuous hyperchunks, preserving textual token order and visual spatial structure. The backbone learns a single vector field over these hyperchunks through Flow Matching. Joint attention enables cross-modal interaction, while modality-specific feed-forward networks process each modality. The model predicts multiple target chunks in parallel during training and generates hyperchunks sequentially at inference. We instantiate MF-1 and pretrain it on multimodal data. Across 0.6B, 1.2B, and 1.6B scales, continued pretraining consistently improves multimodal modeling. With only 150B pretraining tokens, MF-1 achieves an average score of 82.8 across GenEval and DPG-Bench and 75.3 across VQAv2, MMBench, and POPE, remaining competitive with unified models trained on substantially more data. Under matched data, optimization, and parameter budgets, Multimodal Flow further outperforms representative hybrid and discrete models. These results establish continuous chunk-based embedding flow modeling as a new fully continuous paradigm for unified multimodal modeling. The related code and model are publicly released at https://github.com/hustvl/Multimodal-Flow.

**comment/C1：** 18 pages, 5 figures, 10 tables. Code and model: https://github.com/hustvl/Multimodal-Flow

**title/T1：** Ranking-Aware Prompt Optimization for Multimodal Clinical Diagnosis

**abstract/A1：** Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) are rapidly advancing clinical diagnosis, yet their adaptation pipelines remain anchored to accuracy-based objectives. Clinical data are heavily class-imbalanced: a constant-majority predictor can score above 90% accuracy while being clinically useless. We therefore evaluate and optimize for AUROC, a threshold-free score that ranks positives above negatives and is invariant to class balance. We focus on prompt optimization in MLLMs. Reflective methods such as GEPA use a binary scores matrix with one row per evaluation instance and one column per candidate prompt; cells record per-instance correctness, so the column average is accuracy and drives candidate selection. We introduce pair-level Pareto prompt evolution (Ranking-PE), which replaces each correctness row with a pairwise-ordering row over (positive, negative) instance pairs: the cell is 1 if the candidate scores the positive higher than the paired negative. The column average then equals empirical AUROC (by the Wilcoxon-Mann-Whitney identity). We apply this swap at all three layers the prompt evolution search reads from - the scores matrix that decides Pareto dominance, the per-example feedback to the reflection LM, and final candidate selection - at no extra model calls and with no surrogate loss. Across three diseases on MIMIC, accuracy-based prompt evolution can degrade ranking; Ranking-PE reverses this, beating the accuracy-based recipe by +5.8 AUROC pp on fine-tuned Qwen3-VL-8B and +16.2 pp on MedGemma-4B. Ablations examine each design component and show that a medical-grade visual backbone - via vision-encoder-tuned SFT or medical pretraining - is a prerequisite that prompt search cannot replace - our recipe extends reflective prompt evolution from text-only data to multimodal clinical decision-making.
